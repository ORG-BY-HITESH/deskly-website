Who it's for

A real digital wellbeing system for daily Windows life

Deskly is built for people who want more than raw numbers. It helps you understand patterns, lock down distractions, and make healthier screen decisions every day.

Students trying to stay focused

Understand exactly where study time leaks, then use app locks and focus mode to protect your learning blocks from distraction.

Professionals building deep-work habits

See your day on a visual timeline, uncover your biggest attention drains, and run dedicated focus sessions to build stronger routines.

People reducing unhealthy browsing cycles

See browsing behavior clearly, set practical boundaries with limits and locks, and replace mindless usage with intentional time.

Anyone improving digital wellbeing

Use desktop widgets for at-a-glance awareness and weekly reports to reflect and improve. Deskly is designed as a long-term companion, not a one-time utility.
