Widgets that live on your desktop, not over it

Most "always-on-top" widget tools sit above every window, competing for your attention while you work. Deskly does the opposite:

  • Widgets are pinned to your desktop layer — press Win+D or minimize your windows and they're instantly visible.
  • They update every 60 seconds, so figures stay current without needless redraw or distraction.
  • Drag any widget to reposition it, and set a custom background image on the Screen Time, Top Apps, and Top Sites widgets.
  • Turn each widget on or off individually from the in-app Widgets settings screen — use only what's useful to you.

Frequently asked questions

What desktop widgets does Deskly offer?

Four widgets: an analog Clock, a Screen Time gauge showing today's usage against your daily goal, a Top Apps widget showing your 3 most-used apps today, and a Top Sites widget grouped by browser.

Do the widgets float on top of my other windows?

No. Unlike typical always-on-top overlays, Deskly's widgets sit on your desktop beneath your other windows. Press Win+D or minimize everything to see them instantly.

How often do the widgets update?

Every 60 seconds, so the numbers stay current without constantly redrawing.

Can I customize the widgets?

Yes. The Screen Time, Top Apps, and Top Sites widgets support a custom background image, and each widget can be dragged to any position on your desktop.
